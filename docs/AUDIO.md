# Audio and vocals

Use the original source audio as the comparison reference. FFmpeg/ffprobe inspect and extract streams; Audacity handles waveform work; Ableton handles the vocal arrangement; Equalizer APO and MOTIV Mix belong to device/system setup. AudioSep is an available separation environment, not a guarantee that a particular separation sounds acceptable.

The preferred Ableton layout is eight vocal layers plus a separate beat/music track:

1. Lead
2. Room
3. Double L
4. Double R
5. Tuned Shadow
6. Low Body
7. Air Motion
8. Filtered Echo

Keep references muted and separate. A track-layout preference is not approval of EQ, compression, tuning, timing, or the final balance. Avoid duplicating gain changes across clip gain, devices, and faders. Review timing against the unchanged accompaniment and retain an untreated lead for comparison.

For video, distinguish a source-audio copy from a lossy transcode. `media.py export` makes AAC audio for review/delivery clips and does not claim bit-identical audio. Keep a lossless master when needed. Listen to the beginning/end of cuts, transitions, consonants, low-frequency balance, and loud peaks. A limiter or loudness measurement cannot establish listening approval.

Any voice-generation or identity-blending work is a separate consented task. These tools do not recreate, retry, or authorize previously rejected voice treatments.
